Waste Awareness Guide 🌿

Sahi tarike se waste segregate karo — chhoti aadat, badi difference.

🟢

Wet Waste

Kitchen waste, vegetable peels, food scraps, tea leaves, egg shells, garden waste, flowers.

Disposal: Green bin
🔵

Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, glass, metal cans, tetra packs, packaging material, rubber.

Disposal: Blue bin
🔴

Hazardous Waste

Batteries, medicines, syringes, e-waste, paints, chemicals, CFL bulbs, sanitary waste.

Disposal: Red bin / Special collection

Quick Rules 📋

✅ Do's

  • Waste ko source pe segregate karo
  • Wet waste ko compost banao
  • Dry waste ko recycle karo
  • Reusable bags use karo
  • Local kabadiwala ko dry waste do
  • Complaint report karo agar issue dikhe

❌ Don'ts

  • Road pe kachra na phenko
  • Waste ko na jalao — pollution hota hai
  • Wet aur dry waste mix na karo
  • Public places pe littering na karo
  • Single-use plastic avoid karo
  • Drain mein waste na bahao
